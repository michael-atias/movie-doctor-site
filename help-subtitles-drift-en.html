Subtitles that drift out of sync halfway through

Updated: 25 November 2026

The first minutes are perfect. Then, somewhere in the middle, the subtitles start running early or late, and the gap only grows. Shifting every line by one amount will not help here, because the offset is not constant.

The two main causes

1. A different frame rate

If the subtitles were timed for a 25 fps video and yours runs at 23.976 fps (or the other way round), the gap grows slowly and evenly through the whole video. After an hour it can reach minutes.

How to tell: the gap grows gradually, with no jumps.
How to fix: in Subtitle Edit, choose Synchronization, then Change frame rate, convert from the old rate to the new one, and save.

2. A scene added or cut

If your version has a scene the subtitle version did not, everything is on time until that scene, and from there everything is off by the same amount.

How to tell: the gap jumps at one point and stays fixed after it, sometimes at more than one point.
How to fix: Subtitle Edit has Point sync: mark a few lines at the right time across the video and it works out the rest. It works, but it is slow, because you find every point by hand.

Why it is hard by hand

You first have to find out which of the two happened, where, and by how much. In a two-hour video that can mean half an hour of skipping back and forth. If both causes are mixed, it gets harder still.
