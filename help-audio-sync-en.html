Audio out of sync with the lips

Updated: 25 November 2026

You see the mouth move and the words arrive a moment later. Or earlier. Even a fifth of a second is noticeable, and after a few minutes it becomes tiring to watch.

Why it happens

  • Phone videos: phones usually record at a variable frame rate, and some editors and players handle it badly. The sound stays on time and the picture does not.
  • Conversion or trimming: a converter added or removed a small piece at the start of the audio.
  • Separate recording: the sound came from a separate microphone and was lined up by hand.

Fix while watching: VLC

  1. Open the video in VLC.
  2. Sound ahead of the lips? Press K to delay it.
  3. Sound behind? Press J to bring it forward.
  4. Each press moves it 50 milliseconds. It is easiest to tune on words that start with P, B or M.

Permanent fix

To save a file that plays right everywhere, the audio track inside the file has to move. Editors such as the free version of DaVinci Resolve let you slide the audio on the timeline and export again. It works, but you need to know the exact amount, and exporting the whole video again takes time and can cost quality.

If the offset changes along the video, one shift is not enough, and you need to measure it in several places.
