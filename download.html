Download Movie Doctor Pro

Version 1.0.0 · Windows 10/11 64-bit · about 3 GB

The installer comes in 3 files because it is large. Download all three into the same folder, then run the first one.

  1. Download MovieDoctorPro-Setup.exe 3 MB, the installer
  2. Download part 1 about 1.8 GB
  3. Download part 2 about 1.1 GB

After downloading

  1. Run MovieDoctorPro-Setup.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway: the app is new and not yet digitally signed.
  2. When setup ends, the app opens and downloads its AI models once (up to 10 GB, depending on what you choose).
  3. Paste the licence key from your e-mail into the Licence window. No key yet? You get a 14-day free trial.
